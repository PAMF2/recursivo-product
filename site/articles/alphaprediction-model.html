How AlphaPrediction is trained.

The ruler that measures a simulator is the signal that trains a better one. AlphaPrediction only learns from predictions it can check against real people, the same idea that made AlphaFold work.

Most models in this category are graded by the people selling them. AlphaPrediction is graded by real people, on public data, before it is ever allowed to improve. That one rule, learn only from wins you can verify, is the entire design.

The principle

AlphaFold got good at protein folding because folding has a checkable answer. AlphaProof got good at math because a proof can be verified. The pattern repeats every time something compounds, and it is our whole thesis.

Recursion only compounds where the outcome can be checked. Train against a signal you cannot verify and the model drifts. Train against ground truth and it compounds.

Predicting how people behave has exactly that checkable answer, because real people already told us what they think. RecursivoBench turns their answers into a score. AlphaPrediction turns that score into a training signal. The ruler that measures is the ruler that trains.

The training loop

Training is an evolutionary search over predictors, anchored to verified accuracy. It runs on the same measure-and-improve stack behind the benchmark, as a loop whose only reward is a number we can check against reality:

  • Propose. Generate variations of the predictor: how it is prompted, how it reads the person, how it combines answers.
  • Grade against real people. Score each variation with the exact benchmark scorer, on held-out answers. The reward is the verified number, never a self-reported one.
  • Select. Keep only what beats the current best and holds up on a separate holdout. If nothing improves for several rounds, stop, rather than reward noise.
  • Accumulate. Every graded prediction adds to the ground truth, which makes the next round's target stronger.

What the first loop taught us

8items in the first anchor

We ran the loop end to end at small scale, and the honest result is more useful than a hero number. The machinery behaved exactly as designed: it proposed predictors, graded them against real people, and selected on verified accuracy with an early stop. But the anchor held only eight items. One candidate hit 0.50 on the holdout while merely tying on the training anchor, so it was not promoted, and the run stopped after three rounds with no real gain.

The mechanism was right; the signal was thin. Eight items cannot tell a real improvement from luck.

The next move is to grow the anchor to a hundred or more stratified items before evolving further or plugging in heavier simulators, so the reward is trustworthy enough to compound on. This is what training against a verifiable outcome buys you: the loop refuses to promote a candidate it cannot prove is better. A self-reported pipeline would have shipped that 0.50. Ours declined it.

Honesty ships in the response

Predicting one real person is hard for everyone right now, about 0.36 across the board, below the 0.583 you get by always guessing the most common answer. We do not hide it. Every AlphaPrediction response carries its own verified accuracy and a plain caveat, broken down by segment, so you get the prediction and how much to trust it in the same breath.

Ask how a segment would answer something, say whether a 35 to 54 year old in the US Midwest would pay twenty dollars a month for a product, and the answer comes back with the full split across the options, the single most likely choice, and an honest footnote: this is a verified group-level distribution, individual accuracy is about 0.36, so do not read it as a per-person verdict. It names its ground truth too, the wave-4 held-out slice of Twin-2K-500.

The number arrives with its error bar attached. We are the only provider that returns the prediction and its verified confidence in one payload, caveat included, so a buyer always knows how far to trust it.

Why this is the moat

A predictor on its own is a prompt over a language model, and anyone can copy that. The defensible part is the loop. The verified signal that grades everyone is the signal that trains ours: every dollar of Verify traffic sharpens the ground truth, a sharper anchor makes AlphaPrediction better, and a better model pulls in more traffic. That is a loop, not a funnel, and it only closes because the outcome can be verified.
