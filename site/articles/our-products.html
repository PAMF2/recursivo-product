Recursivo starts by measuring and ends by predicting. In between, the same verified ground truth flows through four product lines, each one feeding the next. This is all four, and the loop that binds them into one product.

The loop, in one paragraph

Measure everyone with an open benchmark. Grade any model against real people through an API. Train a verified predictor on that same signal. Accumulate every resolved prediction as proprietary ground truth, then feed it back into the ruler. Measure, grade, train, accumulate, and around again with a stronger ruler each turn. The four products below are the four stations on that cycle.

The loop, not any single model, is the moat. Anyone can copy a predictor. No one else has closed the loop that turns verification into data.

RecursivoBench, the open ruler

The benchmark grades any human-behavior simulator against real people, by segment, with a temporal holdout so nobody scores well by memorizing the past. It leads with the number the category prefers to hide: individual accuracy, not a flattering aggregate. On Twin-2K-500, which covers 2,058 real people across 46 categorical items with wave-4 held out, the best model on the market scores 0.360 at the individual level and loses to a constant guess at 0.583.

That gap is the whole reason the ruler exists. Aggregates flatter; individuals expose. Where it stands: v1 is live, with a multi-model public leaderboard. The full method is in how RecursivoBench works.

Verify, the grader for hire

Verify is the benchmark as an API. It grades anyone's simulator without needing access to the model internals. You send a batch of predictions, tagged with the model name and, for each item, which person answered, the question, and the answer the model gave. It returns honest accuracy back: the individual number, the group-level number, a breakdown by segment showing where the gaps open, and the worst items so you can see exactly where the model fails.

Two sides pay for that. A vendor wants a seal it can show customers; a buyer wants to de-risk a purchase before signing. Every certification also distributes the Recursivo brand into a vendor's customer base. Where it stands: the API is live, with endpoints for submitting predictions and getting a grade.

AlphaPrediction, the verified model

AlphaPrediction is our own frontier predictor, trained and selected against verified ground truth in the spirit of AlphaFold. Individual prediction sits around 0.36 for everyone in the category today. The difference is the training signal: AlphaPrediction uses the ruler itself as its reward, so it can compound instead of plateau.

Its Predict API takes a question and a set of options and returns the predicted distribution over those options, the single most likely answer, and, in the same payload, its verified accuracy with an honest caveat: the group-level distribution is trustworthy, individual accuracy is about 0.36, so do not read it as a per-person verdict. The prediction ships with its error bar attached. Where it stands: Predict API v0 is live. The training loop is described in how AlphaPrediction is trained.

Open Predictions, the compounding archive

Open Predictions is a public registry where predictions, ours and third parties', are logged and later graded against reality when the outcome actually arrives. Every resolved prediction becomes data. Over time this builds the largest verified dataset of human behavior anywhere, a moat that thickens on its own. Think prediction market meets benchmark: open, verifiable, cumulative.

Where it stands: concept, the step after Bench, Verify, and Predict are established. It is where the loop stops being a diagram and starts being an asset no competitor can buy.

Why the loop is the moat

A predictor on its own is a prompt over a language model, and anyone can copy that. The defensible part is the way the four connect. The verified signal that grades everyone through Verify is the same signal that trains AlphaPrediction. Every dollar of Verify traffic sharpens the ground truth; a sharper anchor makes the model better; a better model pulls in more traffic; and every resolved prediction lands in Open Predictions as proprietary data that makes the next ruler stronger still.

That is a loop, not a funnel, and it only closes because the outcome can be verified. Competitors have the generator. What no one else has assembled is the closed cycle of measure, grade, train, accumulate. That is what makes the whole thing one product, and the only honest one in the category.
