Our thesis

Simulating human behavior only has value where the prediction is verified against reality. Today no one verifies it. The ruler that measures is the ruler that trains, and recursion only compounds where the outcome is verifiable.

Whoever owns verified ground truth owns the loop: measure the field, train the model against truth, log every new prediction, grade it against what actually happened. That loop is the company.
